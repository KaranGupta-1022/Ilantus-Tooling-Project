FAQ
1. Can I share passwords with contractors or temporary staff?
keyboard_arrow_down
Yes. StackBob allows you to share specific credentials with external vendors or temporary staff with granular permissions. You can easily revoke access in a few clicks when projects end or contracts expire.
2. Does StackBob support multi-factor authentication (MFA)?
Yes. When you log in via Google or Microsoft, StackBob also requires your personal 6-digit PIN before unlocking the password manager. This means that even if someone steals your account, they cannot access StackBob without PIN.
3. What’s the difference between StackBob and a browser-stored password?
Browser storage is built for convenience — not security. Browser passwords are easily extracted by malware, can't be shared securely, have no audit trails, and offer no administrative control. StackBob provides enterprise-grade encryption, centralized management, and comprehensive security controls that browsers simply can't match.
4. What happens if an employee leaves the company?
Admins can revoke their access instantly without changing passwords or disrupting team workflows. All stored credentials remain secured in the central vault.
5. How do I switch to StackBob?
You can quickly import all existing credentials from your current password manager into StackBob’s encrypted vault without disrupting daily workflows.
